# Yevhenii Pinchuk — Design & Multimedia Portfolio

Selected work in graphic design, packaging, photography, video, websites and creative tools.

The portfolio presents six case studies from work for PLAY THAT SONG, with project descriptions explaining the brief, Yevhenii’s contribution and the outcome. Interactive demonstrations include a card builder, a 3D packaging viewer and a motion graphics tool.

Available in German and English, with CV PDFs in both languages.

## About

Yevhenii Pinchuk is a graphic and multimedia designer based in Halstenbek, near Hamburg. His work connects visual design with technical implementation, from printed packaging and product imagery to websites and workflow tools.
